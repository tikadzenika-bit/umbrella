Before you come

How do I book?

Send your dates on WhatsApp to +995 568 77 17 86. The house answers and confirms the room.

How do I pay?

In cash, in Georgian lari, at the house.

My flight lands at night. Can I check in?

Yes. The front desk is open 24 hours. Tick “I arrive late” in the form so the house knows.

How do I get here from the airport?

Ask for the transfer in the form, or take a taxi: about 20 minutes.

How do I get to the old town?

Walk ten minutes to Isani metro. Liberty Square and the old town are two stops away.

Can under-18s stay on their own?

No. Guests under 18 stay with an adult.

Write to the house about dates, a late arrival or the airport transfer.
